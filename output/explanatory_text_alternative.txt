This line chart shows the percentage of patients meeting the exercise goal across four follow-up visits for the Home-based and Center-based programs. Both programs start at nearly the same percentage during visit 1, with 58% of Home-based patients and 57% of Center-based patients meeting the goal. The percentages increase at each visit for both groups. By visit 4, the Home-based group reaches 70%, while the Center-based group reaches 79%. This represents a 12-percentage-point increase for the Home-based group and a 22-point increase for the Center-based group. At the final visit, the Center-based group is 9 percentage points higher. However, these results do not necessarily mean that the Center-based program is more effective. Patients chose which program they wanted to participate in, so other factors may have influenced the results. Also, each percentage is based on only about 45 patients per group at each visit, so the difference could be due to chance or differences in which patients returned for follow-up.
